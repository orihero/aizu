# AIZU — project description / loyiha tavsifi

Short copy for applications, forms and introductions. Every claim here is true of the
shipped product: six platform engines, read-only by default, one brief per campaign,
one inbox for the leads. Nothing about revenue, customers or traction is asserted.

---

## English

**One line**

AIZU is a lead-discovery agent for social media: you describe what you sell once, and
it returns a short list of ready-to-buy customers every week.

**Short**

AIZU finds customers on social media. You describe what you sell once; AIZU reads
public posts and comments on Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Reddit and Telegram,
and uses AI to pick out the people ready to buy. They arrive in one inbox every week
— each one someone who already asked, in public.

---

## O'zbekcha

**Bir jumlada**

AIZU — ijtimoiy tarmoqlarda mijoz topuvchi agent: siz nima sotishingizni bir marta
yozasiz, u esa har hafta sotib olishga tayyor mijozlar ro'yxatini keltiradi.

**Qisqacha**

AIZU — ijtimoiy tarmoqlar uchun mijoz topish agenti. Siz nima sotishingiz va kimga
sotishingizni bir marta yozib qo'yasiz; AIZU esa Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube,
Reddit va Telegramdagi ochiq postlar va izohlarni o'qib, sun'iy intellekt yordamida
kim haqiqatan ham sotib olmoqchi ekanini baholaydi. Saralangan mijozlar har hafta
bitta umumiy ro'yxatga tushadi — har biri o'z so'zlari bilan. Hech qanday mijozlar
bazasi sotib olinmaydi va notanish odamlarga xabar yuborilmaydi: har bir mijoz —
o'zi, ochiq holda so'ragan odam. Mahsulot veb-panel ko'rinishida ishlaydi:
kampaniyalar, mijozlar, hisobotlar, xarajatlar, jamoa va to'lovlar. Tariflar bepul
rejadan boshlanadi. Toshkentda ishlab chiqilgan.

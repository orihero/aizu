# Archived: 9:16 ad concept (2026-07-22)

Three generated assets from a single ad-concept session: a keyframe image, a voiceover take,
and a vertical video clip.

**Why this is archived, not current.** The concept breaks the non-negotiable content
guardrails in [`../../aizu-promo-video-treatment.md`](../../aizu-promo-video-treatment.md),
in two ways:

1. **It shows the platforms.** The keyframe radiates LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube, Quora,
   Reddit, Threads, and Telegram logos out of the Aizu ping mark. The treatment forbids
   depicting "social platforms or their logos" outright.
2. **The headline names the mechanism.** "Your next customer already commented." says where
   the leads come from and how they were found. The treatment's positioning is a deliberate
   black box — "never say where, never show how" — with the input-to-output gap rendered as a
   single lime pulse.

This work predates the treatment by about a week. It is a superseded creative direction, not
an early draft of the current one.

**Files:** `aizu-ad-keyframe-9x16.png`, `aizu-ad-voiceover.wav`, `aizu-promo-9x16.mp4`.

Kept in case the platform-forward direction is ever revisited deliberately. Not wired into
any current build or doc.

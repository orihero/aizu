**Solution / feature tour (24–103s):** Dashboard → connect accounts → SEO ranking → review management (AI replies) → review requests → audience growth → AI campaign creation → scheduling → analytics → AI chat assistant.
**Payoff + CTA (104–113s):** Happy customers, social love, "Get Started Today at MeetMadison.ai."

## Recurring Animation Techniques (reusable toolkit)
1. **Typewriter text** — search queries, review replies, campaign prompts, the final URL.
2. **Card fan / cascade** — phone mockups and profile screens stack with offset.
3. **Radial pop** — logos and task callouts scale-in around a central subject.
4. **Converge-to-point transition** — a scene collapses to center before the next appears.
5. **Edge-drifting purple arcs/circles** — brand motif bridging scenes, framing the logo.
6. **Count-up counters** — follower/post/analytics numbers roll upward.
7. **Chart draw-on** — bars grow from baseline, line charts draw left→right.
8. **Live cursor clicks** — every product interaction shows an animated pointer + button state change.
9. **Modal + backdrop dim** — review reply and new-campaign flows.
10. **Background color swaps** — light gray (problem/marketing) ⇄ solid purple (product) ⇄ white (outro).
